Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Summary

Stoplume is a set of client-side calculators. Your focal lengths, apertures, coordinates and dates are processed by JavaScript inside your browser and are never transmitted to Stoplume. There is no account system, no login and no user database.
This page nevertheless discloses in full what third parties can collect if and when advertising or affiliate features are enabled, because that is a requirement of the programmes involved and because you deserve to know before you use the site.

Information Stoplume itself collects

None that identifies you. Stoplume runs no analytics of your calculator inputs and operates no server-side computation for the tools.
Preferences you set — language, light or dark theme, and your camera profile (sensor format, custom dimensions, resolution) — are saved in your browser's local storage. Local storage stays on your device, is not a cookie, and is not readable by Stoplume or anyone else remotely. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Third-party cookies and advertising

Stoplume may display advertising supplied by Google, including through Google AdSense. When advertising is active, the following applies:
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Stoplume and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services is described at google.com/policies/privacy/partners.
Advertising partners may also use web beacons (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags or tracking pixels) — tiny transparent images or scripts embedded in a page or an ad — to record that a page or ad was loaded, to count impressions, and to link that event with cookies previously set on your browser.
Because any request to a third-party server necessarily carries it, advertising and affiliate partners will receive your IP address, along with your user agent, screen characteristics, referring page and approximate location inferred from that IP address. Stoplume does not log or store your IP address itself.

Affiliate links

Stoplume participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Stoplume earns from qualifying purchases.
Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and are disclosed where they appear. Clicking one takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy policy then governs. Stoplume receives no personal information about your purchase.

Your choices and rights

You can browse with cookies disabled; the calculators will still work, since none of them depend on cookies. You can clear local storage at any time to reset your preferences.
If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, personalised advertising requires your consent. A consent management platform will be presented before any personalised advertising is served to users in those regions. You may withdraw consent at any time through that interface.
Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because Stoplume holds no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to export or delete; requests concerning data held by advertising partners should be directed to those partners using the links above.

Children

Stoplume is a technical utility intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Stoplume does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated revision date. Continuing to use the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
